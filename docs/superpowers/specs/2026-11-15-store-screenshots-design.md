# Store screenshots: design

Date: 15 Nov 2026. Status: awaiting owner approval.

## Why

Vaulted Money goes live on the App Store and Google Play (€9.99, one-time) and
as a Mac app on Polar. The listing images are what most people judge it by, and
most only see the first two or three in search results. They must sell, in
this order:

1. **Privacy first**: your data never leaves your device (unless AI is
   switched on).
2. **Plain-English search**: type it the way you'd say it.
3. **Ownership** of everything, not just transactions.
4. **Optional AI**: off by default, on at a switch.
5. **Budgets** that warn you.

Success: images that stand out in a finance category full of white, generic
listings, read cleanly at thumbnail size, are honest enough to pass review,
and can be regenerated in minutes whenever the app's screens change.

## Goals

- One command produces every store image from the real app with demo data.
- Both themes appear: the app's light and dark looks are both shown.
- Every size the stores need (below), plus Mac images for Polar/the website.
- Regenerated on each GitHub release, so the images track the latest screens.

## Non-goals

- App preview videos (the existing `marketing/search-demo` videos stay as
  they are; not regenerated by this tool).
- Uploading to App Store Connect / Play Console (done by hand, or later with
  the `asc` skills).
- Windows/Linux images (those builds are a separate piece of work; see the end).

## Anti-goals (a failure even if it "works")

- Misspelled queries, negative net worth, "Infinite" runway or empty areas in
  frame.
- Claims the app doesn't back up (e.g. "never leaves your device" without the
  AI qualifier).
- "Android"/"Google Play" in Apple images, or Apple names (e.g. "Mac") in
  Google Play images.
- Text so small it can't be read in the store thumbnail.

## Look (decided with the owner)

- **Alternating pages**:
  - Odd shots are **dark**: the app's dark brand (navy radial gradient into
    teal), headline white with the teal title gradient
    (`--brand-title-*` dark values) on the key words, light app inside.
  - Even shots are **light**: the app's own light theme (white into
    `--muted` slate with a soft apricot glow), headline in `--foreground`
    navy, key words in the apricot title gradient deepened ~10% for
    legibility, dark app inside.
- **Straight phones** in a slim device frame with a soft glow/shadow.
- **Shot 1 only**: a pair of phones, light and dark, side by side and
  **both fully in view** (neither cut off).
- Headline at the top, sub-lines below it, phone in the lower two-thirds.
  Lines balance so no single word sits alone on a line; each language can
  override a caption's line breaks.
- Every device shows a drawn status bar: "9:41" on the left, "5G" and a
  small battery on the right (no large signal bars). It also keeps app
  content clear of the device's rounded corners.
- Phones run off the bottom edge of the image; the iPad on shot 6 is drawn
  larger so the two panels read at thumbnail size.

## How it gets made

**Approaches considered:**

- **A. Browser-made (chosen).** Playwright drives the real web build with
  generated demo data at each device's size and scale, captures the screens,
  and renders HTML/CSS frames to exact pixel sizes. Fast, deterministic, one
  command, localisable. The iOS status bar is drawn (9:41, full battery).
- **B. Simulator-captured.** Real iOS simulators and an Android emulator, then
  composed with PIL. Most authentic, but slower and fragile, and needs the
  iOS 27.1+ runtime and an emulator. Rejected for the main pipeline; the
  Duo simulator can still be used to check layouts by hand.

**Shape:**

- New tool `marketing/store-screenshots/` next to `search-demo`, reusing its
  Playwright approach (`PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE`, demo data via `/ledgers?demo=1`,
  the "Home Budget" ledger).
- A shot list (JSON) holds route, theme, interactions (e.g. type the query,
  open the donut view, switch AI on), crop/scroll, and caption keys.
- Captions live in a small strings file per language (English first; the
  tool accepts `--lang` for es/zh/nl/ta later).
- `pnpm marketing:screenshots [--set ios|play|mac|all] [--lang en]` writes
  to `marketing/store-screenshots/out/<set>/<lang>/` (gitignored; nothing
  generated is committed).
- The release workflow gets a job that runs the tool and attaches a zip of
  the images to the GitHub release, so each release has screenshots of its
  own screens. Expected cost: a few minutes of CI per release; locally
  about 3–5 minutes for every set.

## Left to the builder

- Exact frame proportions, glow and shadow values per size.
- Scroll offsets and crops so the key element sits mid-frame.
- Pinning "today" for demo data so the numbers are stable between runs
  (demo data already derives from a given `now`).
- A check that fails the run if a capture shows an error state, an empty
  list, or a known anti-goal (e.g. "Infinite", "€-").
